Editing targets
Planning days

A book you flag "Needs planning" always gets a plan-the-chapter day with no word count. Books still being written can also get one at random, so you're not always under word pressure.

Slack

Rest days keep your streak intact. Rerolls let you swap a target you haven't started yet.

Hard mode

Hard mode hands out a list of tasks each day, one per book, and the streak only counts once every task is done. Switch modes from the dashboard. Going back to easy is only allowed once the day's list is finished.

Bonus rounds

Clear the day's real target and you're handed an extra task — and another every time you finish one, until the day rolls over. They never touch your streak, never raise your word level, and the nagging stays off whether you do them or not.
